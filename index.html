02 · Digital Transformation

HR Permission Workflow Digitalization

Corporación COFAR S.A. · Microsoft Teams Approvals

Workflow Digitalization Microsoft 365 HR Operations

The Challenge

Hourly employee permission requests were handled through paper forms. Documents could be delayed, lost or signed without reaching Human Resources. Missing records often led to follow-up and administrative rework at month-end.

My Role & Solution

I independently developed a digital approval workflow using Microsoft Teams Approvals, as part of an effort to digitalize internal processes through Microsoft 365.

  • Established a centralized digital request process.
  • Routed requests to the employee's immediate supervisor, with HR included in the workflow.
  • Directed approved requests to HR for entry into the attendance-tracking spreadsheet.
  • Replaced paper handoffs with structured approval and recordkeeping.

Outcome

The workflow reduced paper usage and helped address lost requests, missing records, approval follow-up and manual reprocessing. It also reduced the administrative workload associated with handling these requests in HR.

Workflow Overview

01 · EmployeeSubmits a permission request in Teams
02 · SupervisorReceives and reviews the request
03 · Human ResourcesReceives approved requests and records them

Key Improvements

  • Less paper handling
  • Improved traceability
  • Fewer missing records
  • Less administrative rework

My Role

Working alongside the product line manager, who coordinated the medical education initiative, I handled marketing and event-support activities.

Observed Outcome

The initial session in La Paz involved approximately six to seven doctors from the relevant specialty. After the event was shared on social media, interest emerged in holding similar sessions in other cities. The initiative was subsequently replicated in three additional cities.
